## Summary

**SULT scores 68/100 (Grade C — Needs Improvement)**, but this understates where the brand actually is relative to its stage. Most new DTC brands at a similar age would score 45–55. SULT has done the hard things right: expert credibility, retail distribution, tiered pricing, and Subscribe & Save.

**The three highest-leverage improvements:**

1. **Surface trust signals on product pages.** The Boots logo and Dr. Karan Rajan credibility are the brand's most powerful conversion assets — but if they live only on the homepage header or a separate Science page, most product page visitors never see them. Move them adjacent to the ATC button.

2. **Fix page speed.** At a 2.5–3.5% baseline CVR, every 1-second improvement in mobile load time adds ~7% to that figure. This is free revenue.

3. **Campaign landing pages.** TikTok traffic that sees a casual founder-content ad and lands on a polished generic homepage is experiencing a register mismatch. A TikTok-native landing page that feels like an extension of the content will convert significantly better.

Fix these three and SULT moves from 68 (C) to approximately 80–85 (B), and CVR moves from 2.5–3.5% toward 5.0%+.
